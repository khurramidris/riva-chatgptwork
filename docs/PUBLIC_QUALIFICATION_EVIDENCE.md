# L12 public-evidence screen — 2026-09-09

Status: **metadata reviewed; no new human outcomes opened; no qualification set
admitted or frozen**. Source availability alone does not establish experimental
relevance, independence, licensing scope for each file, or correct extraction.

The initial product remains aggregate concept/message/scenario comparison. The
first qualification should be narrow enough that the stimulus, human question,
audience and metric mean the same thing for the simulator and human study.
Purchase intention is a stated response, not proof of actual purchasing behavior.

## Candidate requiring a version and instrument audit

The authors' public **Warmth and Competence in Conversational AI** records describe
US Prolific experiments on purchase intention with skincare/laptop chatbot
interactions. Both records display CC BY 4.0. Only their metadata were read:

| Author-published record | Published metadata | Admission status |
|---|---|---|
| [Mendeley v1, DOI 10.17632/df2rgpc836.1](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/df2rgpc836/1) | 6 January 2026; study sizes 198, 200, 400; Gemini-2.5-flash described | Not admitted |
| [Figshare, DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.31004254](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Data_for_b_Warmth_and_Competence_in_Conversational_AI_A_Dual-Route_Model_of_Purchase_Intention_b_/31004254) | 23 July 2026; study sizes 198, 200, 363; a different model described for study 3 | Not admitted |

These are versions of the same research program, not two independent datasets.
The changed sample and model descriptions may reflect a revision; the records
alone do not explain the changes. Before admission, select a precise file version,
verify its hash, codebook, full answer-free instrument, recruitment and exclusions,
and preserve a documented mapping to Rival. Metadata access exposed no participant
answers or effect estimates. No outcome-bearing archive was fetched.

The intervention is a functional chatbot interaction. Treating it as a static
product-description survey would alter what is being measured. This candidate
therefore needs a substantive task-fit review and may be unsuitable for the
current independent-response study path. Do not invent missing chat transcripts,
collapse a multi-item scale to an arbitrary categorical outcome, or use measured
engagement/risk/purchase-intent responses as pre-outcome baseline features.

## Admission sequence

1. Obtain and audit answer-free instruments and source file metadata. Establish
   a relevant, consistent question family and human population. Prefer a directly
   reproducible survey/concept task to an interaction that Rival cannot reproduce.
2. Pin versions and coding rules. Register all prior outcome exposure. Cluster
   related conditions/questions and repeated participants conservatively; they
   cannot be counted as independent replications by renaming identifiers.
3. Keep development, model/distribution fitting, uncertainty fitting, calibration
   and final qualification groups separate. Training outcomes may be inspected;
   final outcomes remain outside the model team's view until predictions are sealed.
4. Define feature extraction, primary metric, tolerance, all four baselines,
   missingness, subgroup scope, full human-sample quotas and cost limits. Check
   the planned number of independent studies can support the statistical gates.
5. Freeze the exact eligible study and participant rosters using the new CLI.
   Public historical data can be a locked retrospective test, but publication
   predating a model's training still leaves contamination risk. A separate future
   human study is required by the saved launch plan.
6. Run the fixed candidate and compare every planned group. Keep negative,
   unevaluable, unsupported and expensive results. A new model or changed protocol
   requires fresh final evidence, not another try on the same exposed outcomes.

This screen deliberately makes no accuracy claim. L12 empirical work remains
open; the new workflow supplies the controls for carrying it out and reporting
the result honestly.
